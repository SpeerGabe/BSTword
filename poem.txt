The sun sets beyond the horizon,
Painting the sky with hues of orange and pink.
A gentle breeze whispers through the trees,
Caressing the leaves with a soft touch.

As night falls, the stars twinkle above,
Creating a celestial tapestry in the heavens.
The moon, a luminous pearl in the velvet sky,
Casts its silvery glow upon the world below.

Nature sleeps, wrapped in the quiet embrace,
Dreaming of the wonders that tomorrow may bring.
Then a sound strikes out into the void,
Signifying the time of 12 o'clock somewhere.

And there sits a clock lonely and in disrepair,
Overgrown and depressed because its caretaker had left.
